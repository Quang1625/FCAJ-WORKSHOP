# Report on “First Cloud Journey & Tech Career Meetup”

### Event Objectives

- Share the journey of learning Cloud computing and developing from a student to an AWS Partner.
- Understand the actual scope of work, responsibilities, and hiring demands for DevOps Engineers in Vietnam.
- Explore Data Analytics roles, career progression paths, and corporate culture at multinational corporations (MNCs).

### Speaker List

- **Danh Hoàng Hiếu Nghị** - From First Cloud AI Journey to AWS Partner
- **Trong H. Truong** - What does a DevOps Engineer really do? Why you interested in DevOps?
- **Dat Pham & Cường Nguyễn** - Real stories to culture in multinational corporations

### Featured Content

#### First Cloud AI Journey
- Introduced an 8-step journey from Student Curiosity to solving real-world problems and Sharing Back to the community.
- Overview of community-building programs like AWS Student Builder Group and AWS Community Builder.
- Provided a roadmap for practicing core AWS foundations such as account setup, cost management, and network infrastructure deployment.

#### Demystifying the DevOps Engineer Role
- Clarified the surging hiring demand and competitive salaries for DevOps Engineers in Vietnam during 2016-2026.
- Contrasted DevOps expectations (only doing CI/CD, IaC, Kubernetes) with the harsh reality (troubleshooting, 24/7 on-call, checking cloud costs).
- Emphasized the importance of fundamental knowledge: Linux, Networking, Programming (Python/Golang), Git, and Containers.

#### Data Analytics & Corporate Culture
- Detailed the daily tasks of a Data Analytics Engineer, including building periodic reports, designing management dashboards, and analyzing operational metrics.
- Identified 4 essential skills for students: Critical Thinking, Communication, Data Storytelling, and Problem Solving.
- Explained 5 stages of career development: Follower, Learner, Problem Solver, System Thinker, and Super Star.
- Introduced cultural traits at MNCs such as "No-Blame Post-Mortem" (focusing on root causes instead of blaming) and "Caring & Inclusive".

### What I Learned

#### Career Mindset
- Understood that in DevOps, copying commands is not the same as truly understanding the system's nature.
- Cultivated the habit of asking "why" before "how" and identifying the true owner of a problem.
- Career advancement requires transitioning from merely executing tasks (Follower) to seeing the big picture and optimizing systems long-term (System Thinker).

#### Engineering Architecture & Culture
- The scope of a DevOps role depends heavily on context, company size, project size, and existing automation systems.
- Good engineering culture in MNCs focuses on protecting reputation and information security through strict standards (like ISO 27001 or SOC 2) for the digital supply chain.
- AI applications should be used to enhance skills rather than stopping oneself from thinking.

### Applications in Work
- Apply the First Cloud AI Journey roadmap to master core AWS services through practical labs.
- Focus on building small projects to self-deploy applications, automate processes, monitor, and fix errors to solidify DevOps foundations.
- Utilize Data Storytelling skills to turn dry numbers into valuable reports that drive business actions.
- Practice the "No-Blame Post-Mortem" culture when analyzing and resolving system errors during teamwork.

### Event Experience

Attending the event provided me with a comprehensive and realistic view of career paths in the IT industry, from Cloud and DevOps to Data Analytics. Highlights included:

#### Learning from Experienced Speakers
- Gained deep insights into the real "headaches" of a DevOps Engineer, from handling complaints about slow systems to optimizing cloud bills.
- Understood how corporate data engineers build dashboards to track order fill rates, fulfillment & last-mile costs, and risk provisions.

#### Networking and Exchange
- Recognized the immense value of joining academic groups like the AWS Student Builder Group to practice skills together, accumulate reward points, and earn AWS certifications.
- Grasped the standard recruitment process at multinational corporations, from automated resume screening (ATS) to direct cultural fit interviews with Leadership.

#### Lessons Learned
- A good engineer thinks systematically rather than focusing on isolated tasks, and knows how to make things clearer and easier for the whole team.
- Technological tools may constantly change in the market, but foundational knowledge and a curious learning mindset will always retain their value.
- To reach the peak of one's career (Super Star), one must not only solve current technical problems but also build strategic vision and train the next generation of system thinkers.
