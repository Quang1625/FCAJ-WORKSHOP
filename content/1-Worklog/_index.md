**On this page**, you will need to introduce your worklog. **How** did you complete it? How many weeks did you take to complete the program? **What** did you do in those weeks?

Typically, and as a standard, a worklog is carried out over about 3 months (throughout the internship period) with weekly contents as follows:

**Week 1:** [Successfully set up a personal AWS account](1.1-week1/)

**Week 2:** [AWS Console,AWS service ecosystem](1.2-week2/)

**Week 3:** [VPC, IAM & MFA](1.3-week3/)

**Week 4:** [NAT Gateway, EICE & Cost Optimization](1.4-week4/)

**Week 5:** [AWS Cloud9 & S3 Static Website Hosting](1.5-week5/)

**Week 6:** [Amazon RDS (MySQL) & Amazon Lightsail](1.6-week6/)

**Week 7:** [Serverless Architecture (Lambda, API Gateway, DynamoDB & Cognito)](1.7-week7/)

**Week 8:** [Security Core, Code Review & MVP Architecture](1.8-week8/)

**Week 9:** [Backend Migration, Cognito Auth & Decoupled Architecture Design](1.9-week9/)

**Week 10:** [S3 Presigned URLs & Word Document Import Lambda](1.10-week10/)

**Week 11:** [Asynchronous Grading Logic with SQS & Lambda](1.11-week11/)

**Week 12:** [Frontend Deployment on AWS Amplify, DNS Setup & Final Delivery](1.12-week12/)
